Chapter 4

Process Costing

Costing a product that never stops coming off the line: departments instead of jobs, equivalent units, cost per equivalent unit, and the production report that ties it all together.

What you'll learn

  • 4.1Process vs job-order costing. When each system fits, processing departments, and conversion cost
  • 4.2Recording the flow. Work in Process by department, and transfers from one department to the next
  • 4.3Equivalent units. Turning partly finished units into whole units with the weighted-average method
  • 4.4Cost per equivalent unit. Beginning cost plus cost added, divided by equivalent units
  • 4.5Assigning costs and reconciling. Cost transferred out, ending work in process, and the cost reconciliation
  • 4.6Operation costing. The hybrid used when different batches share the same operations
