2.2

Predetermined overhead rate

Allocation baseEstimatesFour steps

Why set the rate in advance?

  • Job 214 is finished in March and needs a cost now, for pricing and inventory. The year's actual overhead isn't known until December
  • Actual overhead also swings month to month, so identical jobs would cost different amounts
  • A rate set from estimates before the year gives every job the same fair rate
