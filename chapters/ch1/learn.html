Tough yearWatch the bars: managers trim the discretionary costs, but the committed ones can't move without selling the building or breaking the lease.

"Fixed" only inside the range

  • Northstar's building holds up to 1,500 kayaks a month. Inside that range, rent is a flat $12,000
  • Past 1,500 it needs a second building, and rent steps up to $20,000
  • Anywhere inside the range, the fixed cost stays put. Outside it, all bets are off

Mixed costs: Y = a + bX

Try it · factory electricity
Y = $2,000 + $4 × 1,000 = $6,000
  • a = the fixed part: where the line starts on the cost axis
  • b = variable cost per unit: the slope
  • X = activity; Y = total cost
  • Northstar's electricity: $2,000 a month + $4 a kayak = $6,000 at 1,000 kayaks
