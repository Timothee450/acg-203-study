Chapter 1

Managerial Accounting and Cost Concepts

The vocabulary every later chapter builds on: how managers sort costs, how costs move through a factory, how they behave when activity changes, and which ones matter for a decision.

What you'll learn

  • 1.1Managerial accounting and cost objects. How it differs from financial accounting, and direct vs indirect costs
  • 1.2Manufacturing costs. Direct materials, direct labor and overhead, prime and conversion costs, selling and administrative costs
  • 1.3Product and period costs. Which costs wait in inventory and which hit the income statement right away
  • 1.4Cost behavior. Variable, fixed and mixed costs, the activity base and the relevant range
  • 1.5Costs for decisions. Differential costs and revenues, opportunity costs and sunk costs
  • 1.6Income statement formats. Traditional (gross margin) vs contribution (contribution margin)
