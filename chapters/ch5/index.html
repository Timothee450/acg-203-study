Chapter 5

Cost-Volume-Profit Relationships

How profit moves when price, cost and volume change: contribution margin, operating leverage, break-even, margin of safety, target profit, what-if decisions, the CVP graph and sales mix.

What you'll learn

  • 5.1Contribution margin. The CVP assumptions, unit CM, the CM ratio and the profit equation
  • 5.2Operating leverage. Why a small change in sales can move profit a lot
  • 5.3Break-even and margin of safety. The sales level where profit is zero, and the cushion above it
  • 5.4Target profit. The sales needed to earn a chosen profit
  • 5.5What-if changes. Testing price, cost and volume changes, and structuring sales commissions
  • 5.6The CVP graph. Reading break-even, profit and loss from a graph
  • 5.7Sales mix. Break-even for several products, and what a shift in mix does
