Chapter 3

Job-Order Costing: Cost Flows and External Reporting

Follow a month of dollars through the books: journal entries and T-accounts, the schedules of cost of goods manufactured and cost of goods sold, and what to do with leftover overhead.

What you'll learn

  • 3.1How costs flow. Raw Materials, Work in Process, Finished Goods, Cost of Goods Sold and the Manufacturing Overhead account
  • 3.2Recording the flow. Journal entries and T-accounts for materials, labor, overhead, selling and administrative costs, and completed and sold jobs
  • 3.3The schedules. Cost of goods manufactured, cost of goods sold, and the income statement
  • 3.4Under- and overapplied overhead. Finding the balance and closing it to cost of goods sold or allocating it
